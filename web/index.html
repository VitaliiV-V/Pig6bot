Dynamic pricing

The price of anonymous codes changes with market availability.

  • More available codes → lower price.
  • Fewer available codes → higher price.
  • Every operation recalculates the market.

Price Formula


Price = Pmin × (1 + A × ((100 − Q) / Q))

Pmin — minimum price

A — market coefficient

Q — available codes
