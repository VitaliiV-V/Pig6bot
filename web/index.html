What Pig 7 Gara does

Cooperating bots sharing one config, built to keep a channel's identity, content and admin list under control.

Identity

Channel protection

A rotating name + invisible UUID pair confirms that a post really came from the registered owner, a super user, or a protected channel. Impersonation attempts are deleted on sight, and the UUID rotates after every legitimate post so a captured signature can't be replayed.

Blacklist

EXCOMMUNICADO

A permanent mark that strips a protected channel or user of its protection and adds it to the blacklist. Once applied it's final — the entry is cut off from the network for good, managed by Alpha users from the admin panel.

Media

Media download

Pulls any photo, video, document or voice message posted in the main channel straight to the requesting user, no forwarding required — just reply to the post with the command.

Privacy

Shadow publications

Connect an anonymous channel, publish within your daily quota and manage your subscription in the bot. Author lookups are available within each plan's limits.

Moderation

Content filtering

Off, manual or full lockdown moderation modes, with leetspeak-aware word filtering, per-GIF bans and automatic lockdown if 10+ messages land within 5 seconds.

Emergency

Judgment Day protocol

A dedicated lockdown mode: the channel switches to maximum security and a separate bot deletes incoming and edited messages, including media, without command or confirmation-code exemptions.

Control

Admin panel

An inline dashboard for Alpha users to toggle moderation and Judgment Day, and to browse and manage protected and super users.

Assistant

Jarvis AI

A Gemini-powered assistant that responds when mentioned and runs the same protection pipeline on every message. A designated master user can issue natural-language admin commands to it.
